# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in the SiliconWin community a harassment-free experience for everyone. That applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces, such as issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project maintainers clarify and enforce our standards of acceptable behavior. They take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct. They explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

Report instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers privately:

- through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the repository's **Security** tab › **Report a vulnerability**), noting that the report concerns the Code of Conduct, or
- by contacting a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these guidelines when they decide on the consequences of a violation:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional or unwelcome behavior: a private written warning that explains why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued behavior. For a period of time, the person must avoid interacting with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with those enforcing this Code of Conduct.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
